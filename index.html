Cost methodology

Charts read Epoch’s Training compute cost (2023 USD) field directly. These are final-run compute estimates, not a dashboard cost model. Cloud-rental and upfront-capital fields are not combined with this series.

Costs exclude full R&D, researcher compensation, failed experiments, and other non-final-run costs. Confidence labels are source assessments, not per-point confidence intervals.

Confidence labels

  • Confident: disclosed or strongly supported
  • Likely: inferred from multiple signals
  • Speculative: limited evidence
  • Unknown: no rating assigned

Important caveats

  • Charts load all supplied model rows. Explicit frontier-only charts are labeled. This is a selected snapshot, not the full Notable Models database.
  • Inference costs are not plotted; training cost is one slice of total economics.
  • Open-weight coverage is calculated after loading.
  • Cost estimates are amortized hardware + energy, not full development cost (which includes R&D staff, failed experiments, etc.).

Methodology

Sources, calculations, and limitations

What we count

Historical chart values come directly from Epoch’s Training compute cost (2023 USD) field, interpreted as amortized final-run compute costs. They exclude full R&D, researcher compensation, failed experiments, data acquisition, and other non-final-run costs. The dashboard does not recompute these costs or combine cloud and upfront costs.

What the data shows

General model charts use all supplied records within their stated filters. Cluster scale, indexed model growth, and frontier organization participation explicitly require Frontier model = TRUE.

The 2.4× growth rate

Cottier et al. 2024, revised 2025 report 2.4× annual growth since 2016 (90% CI: 2.0× to 2.9×). Their published extrapolation places costs above $1B around 2027 if the trend continues. This is external context, not a fit or forecast calculated here.

Different sources, different estimates

The external Stanford AI Index estimate of roughly $190M for Gemini Ultra differs from the local Epoch cost field. It is an estimate, not a company-disclosed final bill, and is not plotted. Missing records and uncertain estimates cannot establish actual private spending.
